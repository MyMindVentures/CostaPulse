# CostaPulse — Project Context

## Purpose

CostaPulse is a premium digital platform for discovering, presenting and booking authentic coastal experiences, professional maritime services and local collaborations.

The project was founded by Kevin De Vlieger to bring together his professional maritime experience, practical leadership, watersports knowledge, hospitality, adventure mindset and local service concepts within one coherent premium brand.

## Geographic starting point and expansion

CostaPulse starts from the Costa Blanca and expands deliberately rather than becoming a generic global marketplace.

The first consumer focus is the Spanish Mediterranean coast between Málaga and Jávea. This launch corridor includes carefully selected marinas, coastal towns, beaches and bays suited to Kevin's own private yacht charters, paddleboard mentoring and kayak experiences.

The first expansion regions may include:

- the Balearic Islands;
- the Canary Islands;
- other selected Spanish coastal destinations that fit the CostaPulse quality and brand standard.

Geographic expansion must remain curated. New regions should be added because they offer strong, authentic coastal experiences and reliable operational conditions, not simply to increase listing volume.

## Professional maritime scope

CostaPulse also includes a distinct professional maritime domain for Kevin's commercial expertise and services.

The first professional focus is harbour towage and related maritime work in Western Europe, especially:

- Rotterdam;
- Amsterdam;
- the Scheldt region, including Antwerp, Ghent, Terneuzen and Vlissingen where relevant;
- later, other selected Western European ports.

This professional domain may include harbour tug captain services, ASD tug operations, training captain work, ship-handling mentoring, maritime consultancy, professional credentials and availability for assignments.

The professional area may be more technical and operational in content, but it remains part of the same CostaPulse brand and must never become a visually disconnected corporate subsite.

## One brand, multiple domains

CostaPulse is not three separate brands for tourism, watersports and towage. It is one ecosystem built around Kevin's real capabilities, experience and lifestyle.

The common brand promise is:

- carefully selected quality rather than anonymous volume;
- practical expertise and credibility;
- personal service and authentic hospitality;
- strong maritime and coastal identity;
- premium presentation without excess or pretension;
- experiences and services connected to real places, people and operational knowledge.

## Project principles

- The repository is the permanent knowledge base for the project.
- Supabase is the source of truth for live application and business data.
- GitHub Issues contain concrete implementation work, bugs and feature tasks.
- Documentation contains durable context, decisions, conventions and technical memory.
- No operational customer, booking, partner or crew data belongs in documentation.
- Avoid duplicate documentation and temporary note files.

## Product identity

CostaPulse should feel premium, personal, adventurous, trustworthy and distinctly Mediterranean. It is not intended to look or behave like a generic marketplace or SaaS template.

The CostaPulse identity remains consistent across all locations and product areas. Regional pages may adapt their imagery and editorial emphasis, but they must not introduce separate brands, palettes or disconnected design systems.

Examples of contextual expression:

- Costa Blanca: bright Mediterranean water, marina life, rocky coves, beaches, sun and relaxed hospitality;
- Balearic Islands: refined island character, clear water and premium coastal escape;
- Canary Islands: Atlantic energy, volcanic landscapes and adventure;
- Western European towage: steel, harbour infrastructure, powerful vessels, precision manoeuvring and professional competence.

These contexts change the subject matter, not the underlying CostaPulse look and feel.

## Core domains

- Experiences and services
- Private yacht charters
- Watersports and outdoor activities
- Destinations, marinas, beaches and coastal discovery
- Professional maritime services
- Harbour towage and ship-handling expertise
- Local partners and referrals
- Bookings and payments
- Customer accounts
- Crew and team platform
- Admin and content management

The `/docs` directory is limited to the ten numbered files defined in the repository. New permanent knowledge must be added to the relevant existing document rather than creating another Markdown or text file.
